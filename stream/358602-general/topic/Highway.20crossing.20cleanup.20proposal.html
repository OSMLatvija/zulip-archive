Dimitar155 (Nov 26 2026 at 18:55):

Hi folks,

Just a quick heads-up that voting on the Highway crossing cleanup proposal (deprecating crossing=* except =no and =informal and approving crossing:signals=*) is open until December 2.

https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Proposal:Highway_crossing_cleanup

In Latvia there are ~20k elements with crossing=* which would need updating. It's worth weighing whether deprecating the key fits your local mapping priorities or creates extra cleanup work you'd rather spend on other things first.

More voices from different communities are always helpful, especially since around one third of votes so far come from just the US and Germany combined. No pressure either way, just wanted to share the info.

There are no special requirements for voting. Having a wiki account is enough.
