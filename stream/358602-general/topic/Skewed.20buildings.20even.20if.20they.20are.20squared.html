kaposts (Sep 01 2026 at 08:45):

Not just coordinate precision, some maps simplify the polygons by removing nodes. This can be easily observed on openstreetmap website by switching between standard tileset and shortbread. Usually not a problem but unlucky node removal can sometimes cause visible distortions for shapes that don't have too many corners or when other nearby shapes serve as reference .
